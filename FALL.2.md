# FALL.2 — Search Without Limit

> **Nature of this document.** This is a **regulatory supposition** — a corrective instrument written in the same spirit as `PAYMENTS.WARRANTS.md` (see Part One, *Fall Cause Two — Search Without Limit*). It is **not** a legal order, a finding, a charge, a warrant, or legal advice, and it binds, bills, or names no living person, office, or jurisdiction. It states, in regulatory form, how an *incorrect action* under this fall cause is recognized and corrected. It is written in care of man and in good faith.

> **Momento — Note as Leader of Inventories.** This document stands as the **leader of the Search inventory**: the first entry against which all later entries are checked. The momento it carries, kept for the record: *a search tests a premise; it does not presume its conclusion — and a search that cannot end empty-handed was never bounded.* Where an inventory of searches is kept, this leader is read first, and nothing in the inventory is deemed correct that this leader would refuse.

## 1. The fall this document regulates

**Search Without Limit** is the quiet fall in which the Search stage forgets it is bounded. It feels like diligence while it is actually appetite: it stops testing the premise and begins presuming the conclusion — gathering not to learn but to confirm. This document regulates the recognition and correction of that fall.

## 2. The standard (what *correct* requires)

A search is correct under this document only when:

1. **It proceeds from prior authority** — no search exists without the lawful foundation of `FALL.1`; a search ungated is void.
2. **It is bounded** — proportionate, documented, and limited to what the authority permits.
3. **It is willing to end empty-handed** — its purpose is to test the premise, not to vindicate it; a search that cannot return nothing is not a search but a verdict in disguise.

## 3. Correction of incorrect action

Where a search exceeds the above:

- **Halt and bound.** The search is stopped and re-scoped to what the authority actually permits; anything gathered beyond the bound is set aside and not relied upon.
- **Restore the premise.** The inquiry is returned to *testing*, not confirming; a conclusion presumed is struck.
- **Record the correction** in the Search inventory, with the point at which diligence became appetite, so the drift is caught sooner next time.

## 4. Inventory note (leader entry)

*Leader of Inventories — Search.* Entry 0 (this leader): every subsequent entry must name its bound and its authority. An entry that gathers beyond its bound, or that could never have ended empty-handed, is by this leader incorrect and corrected by re-scoping. Carried for all comes and about all comes again: **proportion is the whole difference between search and surveillance.**
