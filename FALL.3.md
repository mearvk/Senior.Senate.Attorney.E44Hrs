# FALL.3 — Fine Without Measure

> **Nature of this document.** This is a **regulatory supposition** — a corrective instrument written in the same spirit as `PAYMENTS.WARRANTS.md` (see Part One, *Fall Cause Three — Fine Without Measure*). It is **not** a legal order, a finding, a charge, a bill, a demand for money, or legal advice, and it binds, bills, or names no living person, office, or jurisdiction. It states, in regulatory form, how an *incorrect action* under this fall cause is recognized and corrected. It is written in care of man and in good faith.

> **Momento — Note as Leader of Inventories.** This document stands as the **leader of the Fine inventory**: the first entry against which all later entries are checked. The momento it carries, kept for the record: *a fine is a measured remedy, never a source of revenue — and a system that profits from the fault it finds has a reason to find fault.* Where an inventory of fines is kept, this leader is read first, and nothing in the inventory is deemed correct that this leader would refuse.

## 1. The fall this document regulates

**Fine Without Measure** is the fall in which the Fine stage loses its sense of scale: a punishment that exceeds the thing it answers to, or a demand issued where no competent body ever determined one was owed. In its gravest form it is the fall from *remedy* into *revenue* — a system funded by its own penalties. This document regulates the recognition and correction of that fall.

## 2. The standard (what *correct* requires)

A fine is correct under this document only when:

1. **A competent body determined it** — not an assertion, but a lawful determination resting on the foundation of `FALL.1` and the bounded search of `FALL.2`.
2. **It is proportionate** — measured to the thing it answers to, drawn from a lawful schedule of amounts, never exceeding the matter.
3. **An appeal path exists** — the fine is reviewable and reversible, and the one fined may be heard.
4. **It is not revenue** — no part of the system's funding depends on the fine being imposed.

## 3. Correction of incorrect action

Where a fine fails the above:

- **Vacate the excess.** Any amount beyond the measured remedy is struck; a demand with no determination behind it is void.
- **Sever the incentive.** Where a system profits from its own penalties, the correction severs the funding link before any fine stands.
- **Open the appeal.** The one fined is restored to the right of reply and review.
- **Record the correction** in the Fine inventory, with the measure that was exceeded, so scale is kept honest next time.

## 4. Inventory note (leader entry)

*Leader of Inventories — Fine.* Entry 0 (this leader): every subsequent entry must name its determining body, its schedule, and its appeal path. An entry that is a demand without determination, or a penalty that funds its imposer, is by this leader incorrect and corrected by vacatur. Carried for all comes and about all comes again: **a fine is repair, not profit — the moment it pays its maker, it has ceased to be a fine.**
