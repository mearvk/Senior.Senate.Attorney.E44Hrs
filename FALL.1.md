# FALL.1 — Authority Without Basis

> **Nature of this document.** This is a **regulatory supposition** — a corrective instrument written in the same spirit as `PAYMENTS.WARRANTS.md` (see Part One, *Fall Cause One — Authority Without Basis*). It is **not** a legal order, a finding, a charge, a warrant, or legal advice, and it binds, bills, or names no living person, office, or jurisdiction. It states, in regulatory form, how an *incorrect action* under this fall cause is recognized and corrected. It is written in care of man and in good faith.

> **Momento — Note as Leader of Inventories.** This document stands as the **leader of the Authority inventory**: the first entry against which all later entries are checked. The momento it carries, kept for the record: *authority is earned, never asserted — and a foundation claimed is not a foundation held.* Where an inventory of actions is kept, this leader is read first, and nothing in the inventory is deemed correct that this leader would refuse.

## 1. The fall this document regulates

**Authority Without Basis** is the fall in which the Legal gate is treated as a formality rather than a foundation — authority asserted rather than earned. When it falls, every later stage inherits the flaw: search becomes trespass, fine becomes extortion, hold becomes detention. This document regulates the recognition and correction of that fall.

## 2. The standard (what *correct* requires)

An action is correct under this document only when, before anything downstream proceeds:

1. **A lawful foundation is shown, not supposed.** Jurisdiction, competent authority, due process, evidence, and the right of reply are present and recorded.
2. **The burden of proof rests on the one asserting authority** — never shifted onto the person the action falls upon.
3. **The authority is reversible** — able to be inspected, questioned, and withdrawn if its basis fails.

## 3. Correction of incorrect action

Where an action is taken without the above:

- **Stop at the gate.** The action does not proceed; the downstream stages are void because the foundation they inherit is void.
- **Return the burden.** Any burden placed on the person is lifted and returned to the asserter.
- **Record the correction** in the Authority inventory, with the reason the basis was found wanting, so the same fall is caught earlier next time.

## 4. Inventory note (leader entry)

*Leader of Inventories — Authority.* Entry 0 (this leader): every subsequent entry must name its foundation. An entry with no stated basis is, by this leader, incorrect on its face and corrected by return to the gate. Carried for all comes and about all comes again: **no office, instrument, or category supplies a basis that the facts do not.**
