# FALL.1 — Authority Without Basis

> **Nature of this document.** This is a **regulatory supposition** — a corrective instrument written in the same spirit as `PAYMENTS.WARRANTS.md` (see Part One, *Fall Cause One — Authority Without Basis*). It is **not** a legal order, a finding, a charge, a warrant, or legal advice, and it binds, bills, or names no living person, office, or jurisdiction. It states, in regulatory form, how an *incorrect action* under this fall cause is recognized and corrected. It is written in care of man and in good faith.

> **Momento — Note as Leader of Inventories.** This document stands as the **leader of the Authority inventory**: the first entry against which all later entries are checked. The momento it carries, kept for the record: *authority is earned, never asserted — and a foundation claimed is not a foundation held.* Where an inventory of actions is kept, this leader is read first, and nothing in the inventory is deemed correct that this leader would refuse.

## 1. The fall this document regulates

**Authority Without Basis** is the fall in which the Legal gate is treated as a formality rather than a foundation — authority asserted rather than earned. When it falls, every later stage inherits the flaw: search becomes trespass, fine becomes extortion, hold becomes detention. This document regulates the recognition and correction of that fall.

## 1.5 Position of Stake, and the relief of Saint before Man

Before any package is designed, the **Position of Stake** must be read first. By *Position* this document means what `PAYMENTS.WARRANTS.md` registers in Supposition 6 — the determined place a part holds, the standing it is owed, and the stance it is prepared to defend; and by *Stake* it means what a person actually stands to lose or hold in the matter. The rule is one of order: **read the Position of Stake before you design the package.** A package — any arrangement, instrument, or proceeding assembled around a person — built before the stake is read is a package built on an unknown foundation, which is Authority Without Basis by another name. First find what is at stake and whose; only then compose anything around it.

And in that reading, honor the **relief of Saint before Man**. We mean a priority of mercy: the *relief* owed to the person — the saint in them, the irreducible dignity that precedes any role, rank, or group they belong to — is weighed **before** the man is weighed as an actor to be judged. Relief comes first; judgment second. Thus the law, read rightly, reads about **a man and his saints** — the person and the dignity and mercy that attach to the person — **not his unjogging groups of honor(s)**: not the idle, immovable badges of rank, the honors that do not run, the standings a person is merely sorted into rather than the stake they actually hold. A group of honor that does not move with the living person is not what the law is for. *Thus:* the law reads Man and his Saints first, and his unjogging honors not at all as a basis.

This ordering is itself part of the standard below: no package is correct whose Position of Stake was not read first, and no proceeding is correct whose relief of the person was not weighed before the person was judged.

## 2. The standard (what *correct* requires)

An action is correct under this document only when, before anything downstream proceeds:

1. **The Position of Stake is read first.** What is at stake, and whose, is established before any package is designed around it (see §1.5).
2. **The relief of the person is weighed before the person is judged.** Saint before Man: the dignity and mercy owed the person come before any accounting of them as an actor — and never a person's unjogging groups of honor as a basis.
3. **A lawful foundation is shown, not supposed.** Jurisdiction, competent authority, due process, evidence, and the right of reply are present and recorded.
4. **The burden of proof rests on the one asserting authority** — never shifted onto the person the action falls upon.
5. **The authority is reversible** — able to be inspected, questioned, and withdrawn if its basis fails.

## 3. Correction of incorrect action

Where an action is taken without the above:

- **Stop at the gate.** The action does not proceed; the downstream stages are void because the foundation they inherit is void.
- **Return the burden.** Any burden placed on the person is lifted and returned to the asserter.
- **Record the correction** in the Authority inventory, with the reason the basis was found wanting, so the same fall is caught earlier next time.

## 4. Inventory note (leader entry)

*Leader of Inventories — Authority.* Entry 0 (this leader): every subsequent entry must name its foundation. An entry with no stated basis is, by this leader, incorrect on its face and corrected by return to the gate. Carried for all comes and about all comes again: **no office, instrument, or category supplies a basis that the facts do not.**
