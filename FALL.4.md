# FALL.4 — Hold Without Release

> **Nature of this document.** This is a **regulatory supposition** — a corrective instrument written in the same spirit as `PAYMENTS.WARRANTS.md` (see Part One, *Fall Cause Four — Hold Without Release*). It is **not** a legal order, a finding, a charge, a warrant, a detention, or legal advice, and it binds, bills, or names no living person, office, or jurisdiction. It states, in regulatory form, how an *incorrect action* under this fall cause is recognized and corrected. It is written in care of man and in good faith.

> **Momento — Note as Leader of Inventories.** This document stands as the **leader of the Hold inventory**: the first entry against which all later entries are checked. The momento it carries, kept for the record: *a hold is a comma, not a cage — the moment waiting becomes the punishment, the hold has fallen.* Where an inventory of holds is kept, this leader is read first, and nothing in the inventory is deemed correct that this leader would refuse.

## 1. The fall this document regulates

**Hold Without Release** is the cascade's most tempting fall: a pause that quietly becomes permanent, a "pending" that never resolves, a stay that outlives the question it was meant to protect. A hold falls the instant it stops being open to correction, reversal, and release — the instant waiting itself becomes the punishment. This document regulates the recognition and correction of that fall.

## 2. The standard (what *correct* requires)

A hold is correct under this document only when:

1. **Nothing is final and nothing is executed** while the hold stands — the matter remains open to correction, reversal, and release.
2. **A release condition is named at the outset** — a hold with no stated way to end is already a cage.
3. **It is reviewed, not merely maintained** — the question that justified the hold is re-asked, and if it is answered or gone, the hold ends.
4. **The waiting is not the penalty** — a hold may protect; it may never punish by duration alone.

## 3. Correction of incorrect action

Where a hold fails the above:

- **Release.** A hold whose justifying question has resolved, lapsed, or was never stated is lifted at once, cleanly, with nothing left to be executed later.
- **Convert the stay.** A "pending" that has become permanent is converted back into the comma it was meant to be — or ended.
- **Return the time.** Where waiting became punishment, that is recorded as the harm it is, and the correction accounts for it.
- **Record the correction** in the Hold inventory, with the date the hold should have ended, so no later hold outlives its reason.

## 4. Inventory note (leader entry)

*Leader of Inventories — Hold.* Entry 0 (this leader): every subsequent entry must name its release condition and its review date. An entry that cannot say how or when it ends is by this leader incorrect and corrected by release. Carried for all comes and about all comes again: **a system that cannot let go has lost the right to hold at all.**
